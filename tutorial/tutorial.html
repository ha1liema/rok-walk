How to fold an Origami Heart

1. Take a piece of Origami paper (square size)

2. Fold it half both ways

3. Fold one corner inwards into the center

4. Fold the opposite corner upwards so it reaches the top

5. Fold both the left and right side inwards

6. Fold the sharp corners inwards to create straight edges

Now you should be left with an Origami Heart!
